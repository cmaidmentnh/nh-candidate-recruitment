How the estimates are made
  • Machine (vCPUs and RAM) plus GPUs plus the Windows licence at $0.046 per vCPU-hour, at Google's list prices read {{ rates_read_on }} for {{ priced_regions|join(', ') }}.
  • Not counted: disks, snapshots, IP addresses and network traffic, which bill even while a VM is stopped; sustained-use and committed-use discounts, credits and tax.
  • A machine the table cannot price shows rate unknown, never $0, and the totals say what they leave out. Rates can be changed without a deploy with GCP_COST_RATES_JSON.
