Meta billing

What Meta actually charged, against what it reported delivering. They are different numbers and they are supposed to be: Meta bills when the account crosses its billing threshold or at month end, whichever comes first, so a charge rarely matches a calendar month of delivery. Over the life of the account they should converge.
